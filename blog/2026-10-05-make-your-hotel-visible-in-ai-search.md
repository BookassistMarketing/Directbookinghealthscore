## The First Search Is Now a Conversation

A growing share of travel planning no longer begins with a list of blue links. It begins with a question put to an AI assistant: where should we stay near the old town for three nights in February, somewhere quiet with parking. The assistant does not return ten options. It returns a short, confident recommendation, and the hotels named in it win the guest before a traditional search ever happens.

This is the shift from search engine optimisation to what the industry now calls generative engine optimisation. The goal is no longer only to rank on a results page. It is to be the property an assistant chooses to mention when it answers a real traveller's question.

## Why Some Hotels Get Recommended and Others Do Not

An AI assistant recommends what it can confidently understand. It reads the structured, consistent, machine readable information about your property, and it distrusts anything vague or contradictory. The hotels that surface in these answers tend to share a few traits.

| Signal | Visible to AI | Invisible to AI |
|---|---|---|
| Structured data on your site | Clear property, rooms, location, facilities | Facts buried only in images or PDFs |
| Consistency across the web | Same name, address and details everywhere | Conflicting listings the model discounts |
| Genuine, specific description | Answers real traveller questions | Generic marketing copy with no substance |
| Guest reviews and context | Recent, detailed, plentiful | Sparse or years out of date |

None of this requires you to outspend a chain. It requires your information to be clean, structured and consistent enough that a machine can trust it, which is well within reach of an independent that decides to do it.

## Three Practical Steps Before 2027

### Make your facts machine readable

Your location, room types, facilities, policies and prices should exist as structured data on your own website, not only as words inside a hero image. Structured information is the raw material an assistant uses to describe and recommend you correctly.

### Fix your information everywhere it appears

Assistants cross check your own site against your Google presence, your OTA listings and the directories that quote you. Where those disagree, the model hedges or leaves you out. Correcting inconsistent names, addresses and descriptions is unglamorous work with outsized returns, and it is the same discipline that improves ordinary search too, as we set out in [how AI search is rewriting hotel discovery](/blog/how-ai-search-is-rewriting-hotel-discovery).

### Answer the questions guests actually ask

Vague copy tells an assistant nothing. Specific content, on parking, quiet rooms, family suites, late checkout, walking distances, gives it something concrete to match against a traveller's real request. Write for the question, not for the keyword.
