## A New Year Means New Travel Plans

The first weeks of January are when many people sit down with a new calendar and plan their breaks for the year. Holiday allowances reset, the festive season is over and a trip to look forward to is an appealing antidote to the winter months. For hotels, this January booking window is a chance to secure a significant share of spring and summer business early.

The catch is timing. By the time the window opens, the decisions that shape your results should already have been made. Offers, rates, campaigns and website content all take time to prepare, and the weeks before Christmas are the last quiet moment to get them ready.

## Why Early 2027 Bookings Are Worth Winning Direct

A booking made in January for a stay in April or June gives you something valuable: time. It improves your view of demand, helps with staffing and pricing decisions and brings in revenue well ahead of arrival.

### Early bookers plan carefully

Guests booking months ahead tend to research thoroughly. They read reviews, compare channels and look for a reason to choose one option over another. A clear direct offer gives them that reason.

### The OTAs will be ready

Online travel agencies run strong new year campaigns. If your own website has nothing new to say in January, guests who find you inside those campaigns have little reason to visit you directly.

## Know Your Key 2027 Dates

Start by mapping the dates that matter to your guests. Easter falls early in 2027, with Easter Sunday on 28 March, so spring demand will be pulled forward compared with a later Easter. Add your local events, school holidays in your key source markets, public holidays and any conferences or festivals that fill your destination.

| Period | What guests are planning | Offer idea |
|---|---|---|
| Late winter breaks | Short escapes in February and early March | Midweek stays with added value |
| Easter | Family trips around the school holidays | Family rooms with breakfast and activities |
| Spring weekends | City breaks, events and celebrations | Two night packages with dining |
| Summer | The main holiday of the year | Early booking rate for longer stays |

## Prepare Your Offer Before Christmas

### Create a clear early booking incentive

An early booking rate, a free extra night on longer stays or added value such as breakfast or parking gives guests a reason to commit now. Set clear conditions and a deadline so the offer encourages action.

### Make flexibility visible

Guests booking far ahead often worry about plans changing. Clear, reasonable cancellation terms on your direct rates can tip the decision your way, especially if they compare well with other channels.

### Refresh your website for the new year

Update your homepage, imagery and offers so the site speaks to 2027 from the first day of January. Feature your key dates and link straight to availability with the offer applied.

## Plan Your January Campaigns Now

### Start with the guests you already know

Past guests and newsletter subscribers are your most likely early bookers. Plan a new year email that reaches them before the wider campaigns begin. A platform such as [Bookassist Intelligence](https://bookassist.com/intelligence) helps hotels segment guests and trigger the right message at the right point in the journey, so a family that stayed at Easter hears about Easter again.

### Have paid campaigns ready to launch

Search, metasearch and social campaigns built and approved in December can go live as soon as demand picks up, rather than days later. Plan the budget for this period in advance, as we set out in [building your 2027 direct booking budget](/blog/building-your-2027-direct-booking-budget).

### Measure from the first week

Track the bookings, revenue and booking window for each campaign through January, and compare the share that came direct. Those results show which offers and audiences to back as the year goes on.
