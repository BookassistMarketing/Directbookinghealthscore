## The Most Valuable Inbox Moment You Are Not Using

Once a guest has booked, they are excited about the trip and actively planning it. They open messages about their stay, read them closely and act on useful suggestions. Few moments in the Guest Journey offer such attention, yet many hotels send nothing between the confirmation and the day of arrival.

A hotel pre arrival email fills that gap. Done well, it helps the guest prepare, answers questions before they become phone calls and presents relevant extras at the moment the guest is most receptive to them.

## Why Pre Arrival Emails Outperform Other Campaigns

Unlike a newsletter sent to your whole database, a pre arrival message reaches someone who has already chosen you and has a confirmed date. The context does most of the work.

### They are expected and welcome

Guests look for information about their stay: directions, parking, check in times, what to pack. A message that answers those questions is useful rather than promotional, so it earns high engagement.

### They sell what guests already want

A couple celebrating an anniversary may well want a room upgrade or a bottle waiting in the room. A family may want an early check in. Offering these before arrival captures revenue that would otherwise be lost or left to chance at the front desk.

### They build a direct relationship

Every guest who books through an OTA arrives as someone else's customer. A pre arrival email with a clear, helpful voice is often your first real conversation with them, and the start of a relationship that can bring them back direct next time.

## A Simple Pre Arrival Sequence

| Timing | Purpose | Example content |
|---|---|---|
| Straight after booking | Reassure and confirm | Booking summary, contact details, a warm welcome |
| Around two weeks before | Inspire and upsell | Room upgrades, dining, spa or experience packages |
| Three to five days before | Prepare the stay | Directions, parking, check in times, local tips |
| The day before | Final welcome | What to expect on arrival, a last chance for extras |

Short stays booked close to arrival can compress this into one or two messages. The principle stays the same: each email should be useful first and commercial second.

## Personalisation Is What Makes It Work

A generic message offering a spa package to every guest is quickly ignored. The results come from matching the offer to the stay.

### Use what you already know

Length of stay, room type, number of guests, the reason for travel and previous visits all point to relevant offers. A returning guest can be welcomed back by name and offered the room they chose last time. A business traveller might value a late checkout more than a dinner menu.

### Connect your data sources

Personalisation depends on bringing booking, guest and stay data together in one place. A platform such as [Bookassist Intelligence](https://bookassist.org/intelligence) helps hotels segment guests and trigger the right message at the right point in the journey, without building each campaign by hand. ### Measure more than opens

Track the revenue each message generates, the upgrades accepted and the questions it prevented. Those figures show which offers work for which guests, so every season's sequence performs better than the last.

## Getting Started Before the Winter Season

You do not need a complex programme to begin. Start with one well written pre arrival email containing practical information and a single relevant offer, measure the response, then add further steps. The festive and winter season, with its celebrations and short breaks, is an ideal time to test what your guests respond to.
