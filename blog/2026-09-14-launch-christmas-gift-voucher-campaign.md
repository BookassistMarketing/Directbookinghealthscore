## The Festive Window Opens in September

By the time a guest is thinking about Christmas, the hotels that will profit from it have already been selling for weeks. Gift voucher demand builds from late September, peaks in the fortnight before Christmas, and disappears on Boxing Day. A campaign that goes live in December has missed most of its own season.

Starting now gives you three things a late campaign never gets: time to build a clean voucher product page, a full run at the corporate gifting market that plans ahead, and several weekends of organic social reach before every other hotel is shouting at the same time.

## Why Vouchers Are the Purest Direct Revenue You Sell

A gift voucher is money in the bank today for a stay that may not happen for months, if it happens at all. No OTA touches it, so no commission is deducted. The guest who redeems it arrives already paid, often brings a companion, and typically spends on dinner, spa or a room upgrade once they are with you.

| Revenue source | Commission paid | Cash timing | Redemption behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| OTA booking | 15 to 25 per cent | On or after checkout | Rate only, price sensitive |
| Direct room booking | Card fees only | On or after checkout | Rate plus some extras |
| Gift voucher | None | Immediately, months ahead | Often upsells and brings a guest |

A meaningful share of vouchers are also never redeemed, and every unredeemed voucher is revenue you keep with no cost of service at all. Treat that as a bonus rather than a plan, but it is real.

## Getting the Campaign Live

You do not need a new system. You need a clear product, a visible place to buy it, and terms that protect you.

### The product

Sell experiences, not just monetary values. A named "Festive Overnight for Two" with breakfast converts far better than an empty amount field, because it removes the guesswork for the buyer. Offer two or three fixed packages alongside a flexible cash value for people who prefer to choose.

### The placement

The single biggest cause of weak voucher sales is a buy button buried three clicks deep. It belongs in your main navigation, in your homepage hero for the season, and in the footer of every confirmation and pre stay email you already send. If a guest has to search for it, they will not buy it.

### The terms

Set a validity period of twelve months, exclude your few genuine blackout dates, and state clearly that vouchers are redeemable against any stay. Simple, generous terms sell more than restrictive ones, and they generate fewer support emails in January.
